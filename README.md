# CAMPUS BITE 🍔
### Skip the Queue. Pick Up Fresh.

CAMPUS BITE is a smart campus food ordering platform that makes dining on campus faster and more convenient. Students can explore multiple food outlets, browse menus, check availability, place orders, schedule pickup slots, and track order progress from one platform.

By combining digital queue management and QR-based pickup, CAMPUS BITE helps reduce waiting time during busy college hours. Dedicated vendor and admin tools help manage orders, menus, inventory, and daily operations.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Multi-outlet discovery** – Explore food stalls and canteens across campus.
- **Digital menu** – Browse food items, prices, categories, and availability.
- **Online ordering** – Add items to a cart and place orders conveniently.
- **Order tracking** – Follow order progress from placement to collection.
- **Pickup scheduling** – Choose a convenient pickup slot.
- **Queue management** – Reduce crowding and long queues at food counters.
- **QR-based pickup** – Support quick order verification and collection.
- **Vendor management** – Manage menus, incoming orders, and stock.
- **Admin dashboard** – Monitor outlets, orders, and platform operations.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To transform the traditional campus food experience into a seamless, smart, and time-saving digital experience for students and food vendors.

## 🧭 How It Works

1. Students open CAMPUS BITE and choose a campus food outlet.
2. They browse the menu and add available items to their cart.
3. They place the order and select a pickup slot, where available.
4. They track the order status.
5. They collect their food using the order's QR code, where supported.

## 🏫 Designed For

- College students who want a faster way to order food.
- Campus canteens, cafés, and independent food stalls.
- Campus administrators who need better visibility into food-service operations.
